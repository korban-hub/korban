order, every term under its heading, your "why it matters" notes kept where
you gave them. A term is matched as a phrase, any capitalization, any form of
the word (erect / erected / erection / erecting all hit "Erection").

Where the same term appeared in two sections it lives in one place, so it is
not reported twice. The moves are listed at the bottom. Everything here can

## The ten questions
Shown on every scrub as a checklist you answer (Yes / No / Unclear + note),
separate from the keyword hits:

1. Is scaffold explicitly included or excluded?
2. Who provides scaffold materials and labor?
3. Who is responsible for engineering and stamped drawings?
4. Who pays for scaffold modifications and relocations?
5. Who is responsible for inspections and tagging?
6. Is scaffold shared with other contractors?
7. Who pays for extended scaffold rental or standby time?
8. Are multiple mobilizations included?
9. Who is responsible for damage caused by other trades?
10. Does the contract require scaffold beyond the original drawings or bid specifications?

## Two documents, two sets of marks
Before a scrub you say what the PDF is. The choice picks which categories
run, in what order, and what the four marks are called.

**Scope of Work** (before you price) — runs Scaffold terms → Hidden
obligations → Install, maintain, remove → Shared scaffold → Duration &
schedule → Safety → Engineering. Commercial terms sit out.

| Mark | Means |
|---|---|
| **Price it** | It's ours; carry it in the number |
| **Exclude it** | Not doing it; goes in the exclusions |
| **Assess** | Can't tell yet; RFI before bid day |
| **By others** | The document gives it to someone else; note it so it stays theirs |

**Subcontract** (after you've bid) — runs Commercial terms → Hidden
obligations → Duration & schedule → Shared scaffold → Install, maintain,
remove → Scaffold terms → Safety → Engineering.

| Mark | Means |
|---|---|
| **Red flag** | Cost or liability exposure — including terms that make us pay for things outside our control or for other people's mishaps (indemnification, back charges for other trades' damage, liquidated damages for delays that aren't ours, "responsibility for damage" however caused) |
| **Not in our bid** | Work the document asks for that the number didn't carry |
| **Assess** | Ambiguous; raise before signing |
| **Protects us** | Language that holds our scope where we put it |

Same four colors in both sets. The names and the category order per
document are editable in Backend → Scope keywords.

The search-first fifteen are shown lit — an orange border and glow around
the term — not starred.
